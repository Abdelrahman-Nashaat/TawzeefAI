# TawzeefAI — Requirements and Gaps from the Two Project Documents

This review compares the requirements with the local workspace files as of 29 October 2026. It is not proof that the application or tests passed. Files may change during parallel work; recheck them when implementing and delivering the project.

## Update to the Review

The table below records an earlier snapshot and does not by itself reflect the latest implementation changes. Since that snapshot, a LangGraph path with fixed stages, a human review checkpoint, and persisted resumption has been added, along with a résumé section excerpt parser, a weighted assessment based on HR judgments, and separate conversations for each account. Local appointment selection and calendar-file export became available, and ordinary search uses lexical BM25. PostgreSQL and pgvector run in Docker, and an optional experimental hybrid path exists for added policy documents only. This does not mean the architecture is complete: primary application data remains in SQLite, hybrid search needs expansion and independent evaluation, and actual calendar and email integration, the Streamlit interface, and other requirements need work and verification. Consult the README and tests for evidence and limitations on the current state.

## Data and Evaluation Metrics

- Slide 13 suggests verifying Kaggle and Hugging Face dataset names and licenses before downloading or using them, and preparing a 15–25-page handbook, 5–8 similar jobs, 30 synthetic Egyptian Arabic/English résumés, an anonymized hiring-pattern file, 30 policy question pairs, and 10 ranking cases. `data/capstone_samples/` contains synthetic samples in the target quantities, including 15 sections with logical page numbers rather than a visually rendered handbook PDF. Labels are drafts not approved by a human reviewer and do not constitute an independent test set or real organizational data.
- Slide 15 specifies at least 8/10 for ranking, Q&A accuracy on 30 questions, a candidate-token test against an HR endpoint, and timing against a fully manual baseline for 30 CVs. The booklet calls its success measures **suggested**. Reports must state sample sizes, how answers were evaluated, evidence, and limitations, and must not claim a human measurement that did not occur.
- `evaluate.py` evaluations test synthetic examples and proposed judgments; they are not an independent benchmark or proof of absence of bias or leakage. Human review of the reference, near-miss examples and synonyms, failure analysis, and data held out from tuning are needed; 403 cases alone do not prove isolation of all data.
- The slides propose a six-week plan and division of tasks. This is a team schedule, not evidence that any phase was completed.

## Dependencies and Decisions Requiring Verification

- Choose LangGraph and Streamlit versions, PostgreSQL with a vector store, and embedding, BM25, and reranking libraries compatible with Python, available resources, and model licenses. Do not assume libraries or models are installed until installation and tests succeed.
- Determine real Calendar and Email interfaces, credentials, test environment, permissions, and costs before sending. Adapters can be developed and tested with fakes while clearly disclosing that actual integration has not been tested.
- Real organizational accounts and content, a human-reviewed evaluation reference, and manual screening time are not supplied by the PDF alone. The rest of the application can be developed with synthetic data; do not invent consent for candidate data or a time-saving result.
- The presentation details differ from the earlier local stack choice. This document corrects the impression that `docs/requirements.md` alone covers the slide specifications. The project is now in an independent local `TawzeefAI` repository; local files do not prove that they were pushed or that implementation is complete.
